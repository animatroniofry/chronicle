# Chronicle — D&D 5th Edition Character Sheet

A feature-rich browser app for D&D 5e players and Dungeon Masters. Runs entirely in your browser — no installation, no account, no internet required. All data saved locally.

## Features

- **Character Sheet** — Ability scores, skills, saving throws, HP, AC, death saves, conditions, exhaustion, inspiration, and backstory
- **Combat & Skills** — Skill checks, passive senses, concentration tracker, bardic inspiration, damage resistances
- **Equipment** — Weapons, armor, inventory with category filters, encumbrance tracking, currency, and magic items
- **Spellbook** — Spell slots, cantrips, leveled spells with upcast support, multiclass slot calculator, pact magic
- **Features & Abilities** — Class features, racial traits, feats, background features, and active abilities
- **Wild Shape** — Druid beast form tracker with independent HP and stats
- **Battle Tracker** — Initiative order, HP tracking, conditions, drag-and-drop reordering
- **Dice Roller** — Roll any die (d4–d100) with advantage/disadvantage, modifiers, and roll history
- **Notes & Journal** — NPCs, quests, journal entries, lore notes, bestiary with search
- **Time & Calendar** — In-game clock, Forgotten Realms / Greyhawk / Standard calendar, weather, seasons
- **Companions** — Familiars, mounts, pets, summons, undead thralls with full stat blocks
- **Achievements** — 100+ unlockable challenges across multiple categories

## How to Use

1. Download the app and keep all files in the same folder
2. Open `index.html` in **Google Chrome**
3. Press **F11** for full-screen mode — your entire monitor becomes the character sheet

## Your Data

All data is saved automatically in your browser's local storage. Use **Export** to back up your character as a `.json` file and **Import** to restore it.

> ⚠️ Clearing your browser's site data will erase local storage. Export your character regularly.
